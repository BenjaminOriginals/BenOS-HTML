| Version | Supported          |
| ------- | ------------------ |
| Beta1   | :white_check_mark: |
| Beta0.1 | :x:                |
| Sierra  | :x:                |

## Reporting a Vulnerability

Report any vulnerabilities to us directly by contacting our business email
here: contact@benjaminoriginals.com
We typically get back to you within 24-72 hours and start working on fixing
bugs, vulnerabilities, and other problems as soon as we initially contact you
back. We will then follow up as soon as your request is fulfilled or if we
find it invalid.

## Keeping your account safe

In order to ensure your security, please be sure to follow these steps to keep
your account safe.

* **Step 1:** Set up a secure passcode that you can remember on your own. If you
  can't remember your passcode on your own, set up a hint if needed (in Settings).
* **Step 2:** Do not reuse your passcode across other services. BenOS Connect keeps
  you very secure on the platform, but if your password is on another platform that
  is not managed by Benjamin Originals or secured by BenOS Connect protocol, we
  have no control over keeping it safe, which could put your account at risk.
